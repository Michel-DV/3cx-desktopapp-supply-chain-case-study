## Source handling

- Claims that changed during the incident are resolved in favor of later incident-response findings.
- Attribution labels are kept in the confidence language of the source that uses them.
- Exposure to an affected DesktopApp build is not treated as equivalent to targeted second-stage compromise.
- The report does not reproduce functional malware, credentials, private keys, or intrusive exploitation instructions.
